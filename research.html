Research & publications

The scientific foundation behind my diagnostic and analysis work: aero-engine condition monitoring, developed at TU Delft in collaboration with KLM Engine Services.

Doctoral research

My PhD was a collaboration between TU Delft and KLM Engine Services, built around GSP, the Gas turbine Simulation Program owned by the Netherlands Aerospace Centre (NLR). It focused on using measured engine data to estimate the health of individual engine components, supporting condition-based maintenance. The work produced one journal paper and three conference papers.

PhD dissertation

Gas path analysis for enhanced aero-engine condition monitoring and maintenance
Delft University of Technology, 2017
