About EmElVee

EmElVee Engineering is the independent practice of Dr. Michel Verbist: an aerospace engineer (TU Delft) with a background spanning aerospace research, the defence industry and high-tech software.

The common thread is analytical rigour applied to real problems: understanding how a system behaves, finding what matters, and recommending what to do about it. Based in Curaçao, working with clients locally and remotely.
